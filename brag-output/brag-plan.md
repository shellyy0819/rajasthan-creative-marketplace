# Brag Plan — Craftology

## 9-question rubric

1. **What is the app?** Craftology — a Next.js marketplace for handmade products and local creative workshops across India. Browse artisan goods, book workshops, buyers/sellers/admins with roles, city-based discovery.

2. **Funniest/most impressive claim:** "Discover the Craft. Meet the Maker. Learn the Art." paired with the maker CTA: "There's room for your craft here." — earnest, warm, not a joke product. Impressive bit: trending algorithm mixing views + contacts + likes + artist visits, one product per artist.

3. **Visual hook:** The hero-art block — a warm terracotta/sage arched illustration (sun circle, two arches, a pottery emoji centerpiece, rotated "LOCAL LOVE" sticker badge) on cream paper background. Distinct earthy palette: `--paper:#f8f4ee`, `--cream:#eee5d8`, `--green:#405746`, `--orange:#c66b43`. Serif display font (Playfair Display) + DM Sans body.

4. **What to show from real UI:** Hero section (headline + arch illustration), the product grid cards (handmade symbol emoji on colored visual tiles, price, add-to-cart), the craft-tabs/collection explorer (circular category art), and the workshop list (date-tile + workshop card).

5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 18 seconds. Hook → hero reveal → product grid → workshop card → city/maker CTA outro.

6. **Tone:** preset `polished` — earnest craft-marketplace product film, quiet premium, matches the handmade/artisan brand voice. Direction: "quiet premium look at a thoughtfully built India-wide craft marketplace."

7. **Audio:** warm, soft acoustic/folk-leaning bed, understated — no aggressive drops. One gentle UI tap/click SFX on the add-to-cart beat. Fade out clean on outro.

8. **Share caption:** "Built Craftology — a marketplace where independent Indian artisans sell handmade goods and host hands-on workshops, city by city."

9. **User flow worth showing:** Land on hero → browse the craft explorer / product grid (handmade finds) → check a workshop with a date tile → see the "I'm a maker" / city links outro. (Landing-page + catalog browse; no live checkout flow needed for the brag.)

## Notes

- No secrets/PII brought in. Skipped `craftology.pem`, `client_secret_*.json`, `.env*` per inspection rules.
- No live product/workshop names from the (empty) seed DB were available — storyboard uses the site's own copy and generic card shapes (colors/emoji from CSS), not fabricated real listings.
- Fonts: Playfair Display (serif/display), DM Sans (body/UI).
- Palette: paper `#f8f4ee`, cream `#eee5d8`, green `#405746`, orange `#c66b43`, ink `#252a24`.
